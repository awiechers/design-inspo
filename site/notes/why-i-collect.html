Why I collect beautiful things ✦

August 2026

Taste is a muscle. Every screenshot you save, every poster you stop and stare at, every layout that makes you whisper "who did this" — that's a rep.

The algorithm shows you what's popular. A collection shows you who you are. That's the whole reason this site exists: my bookmarks folder is a self-portrait, and I'd rather hang it on a wall than let it rot in a tab.

Everything I save lives at /inspo. Go poke around.
