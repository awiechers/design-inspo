About ✦

I'm Ana — a designer-at-heart and relentless curator of visual inspiration.

By day I work at the intersection of product and aesthetics; by night I fall down rabbit holes of mid-century Swiss posters, cloisonné fridge magnets made with AI, vintage annual report covers, and yearbook design from decades past.

This site is my public notebook: Design Inspo is where I keep everything that catches my eye, and Notes is where I try to make sense of it all.
